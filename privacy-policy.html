Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Website: https://sahilwebkit.com

1. Overview & Zero-Upload Local Processing

At SahilWebKit (accessible at sahilwebkit.com), protecting visitor privacy is our highest priority. Unlike traditional online converters, our document and image utilities—including the Image KB Reducer, PDF Merger, and Images to PDF Converter—execute 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

Your files are never uploaded to, stored on, or viewed by our servers. All financial and engineering inputs entered into our Solar, SaaS Metrics, and EV Charging calculators stay strictly inside your active browser session.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration, passwords, or payment details to use any calculator or file tool on SahilWebKit. We only receive personal information (such as your name and email address) if you voluntarily send us an email via our Contact page, which we use solely to respond to your inquiry.

Like standard websites hosted on global edge networks (Cloudflare), basic non-identifying technical logs—such as browser type, device category, referring page, approximate country/region, and timestamp—may be processed automatically to prevent abuse, mitigate DDoS attacks, and monitor site performance.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To keep all utilities on SahilWebKit free to use, we partner with Google AdSense and Google-certified third-party ad networks to display advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as advertising cookies and local browser storage) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to sahilwebkit.com or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads to visitors and measure ad performance and invalid traffic.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (My Ad Center). Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Visitors located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or California have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal correspondence shared with us, as well as the right to withdraw or manage cookie consent preferences through our consent notice or browser cookie settings.

5. Children's Information

SahilWebKit is designed for general audiences, engineers, students, and business operators, and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please reach out via our Contact page or email us directly at support@sahilwebkit.com.
