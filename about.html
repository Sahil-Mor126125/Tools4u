About the Platform

About SahilWebKit

Fast, private, zero-upload web utilities and quantitative calculators.

Our Mission

SahilWebKit (sahilwebkit.com) was created to solve two everyday frustrations on the modern web: bloated online file converters that force users to upload sensitive PDFs and photos to unknown remote servers, and overly simplistic calculators that ignore real-world engineering and financial losses.

Every utility on SahilWebKit runs directly inside your browser—with no sign-up walls, no watermarks, and zero server-side file retention.

What You Can Do on SahilWebKit

Engineering & Financial Calculators

Off-grid Solar & LiFePO4 battery sizing, SaaS LTV:CAC & payback modeling, and EV home-charging cost comparisons built with realistic efficiency factors.

100% Local Browser File Studio

Compress JPEG/PNG images to exact KB targets, merge multi-page PDF documents, and convert scanned images into clean PDFs completely offline in your browser memory.

Who Builds SahilWebKit

SahilWebKit is developed and maintained by Sahil Mor, an Electronics & Communication Engineering student and web developer based in Haryana, India. Combining hands-on experience in power electronics, battery systems, and lightweight client-side web architecture, Sahil built this suite to deliver instant, mobile-friendly tools for students, founders, homeowners, and professionals worldwide.
