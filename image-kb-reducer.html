Complete Image Optimization Guide

How Exact-KB Image Compression Works & How to Keep Photos Sharp

Modern smartphone cameras capture 12-megapixel to 48-megapixel photographs that routinely weigh between 3 MB and 8 MB. While high-resolution files are great for printing, almost every online immigration portal, passport application, university admission form, and e-commerce storefront enforces a strict maximum file size—often 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, or 200 KB. SahilWebKit solves this problem directly inside your web browser without uploading sensitive personal documents to third-party servers.

1. Binary-Search Quality Optimization (No Guesswork)

Most basic image compressors give you an arbitrary "Low / Medium / High" slider, forcing you to guess repeatedly until your file fits under an upload cap. Instead, SahilWebKit uses a 9-step binary search algorithm on an isolated HTML5 <canvas> element. It tests compression quality levels between 5% and 96% in milliseconds, finding the highest possible visual quality whose encoded byte count stays strictly below your chosen kilobyte limit.

If you choose an aggressive target like 20 KB on a massive 4000×3000 pixel photo where even minimum JPEG quality exceeds 20 KB, our engine automatically steps down pixel dimensions proportionally until the file fits cleanly underneath your ceiling.

2. Common Portal & Web File Size Requirements

  • USCIS & US Visa / DS-160 Photos: Must be JPEG format, square aspect ratio (600×600 px to 1200×1200 px), and strictly under 240 KB (use our 200 KB preset + 600×600 px option).
  • UK HM Passport Office Digital Photos: Must be at least 600×750 px, JPEG format, and between 50 KB and 10 MB.
  • Government Exam & ID Portals (UPSC, SSC, Banking): Typically require passport photos between 20 KB and 50 KB and scanned signatures between 10 KB and 20 KB in JPG format.
  • Shopify, WooCommerce & Core Web Vitals: Keeping blog and product images under 100 KB in WebP format dramatically improves Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) and mobile SEO rankings.

3. JPEG vs. WebP vs. PNG: Which Output Format Should You Choose?

Format Compression Type Best Use Case Exact-KB Behavior
JPEG (.jpg) Lossy (Adjustable Q) Passport photos, visas, govt forms, portraits Hits exact KB targets with high clarity
WebP (.webp) Modern Lossy + Alpha Websites, blogs, Shopify & WordPress speed 25–35% sharper than JPEG at the same KB
PNG (.png) Lossless Logos, UI screenshots, transparent icons Scales pixel dimensions down to hit KB target

Pro Tip: How to Keep Faces & Scanned Signatures Crisp at 20 KB – 50 KB

If you compress a 4000-pixel-wide photo directly down to 20 KB without resizing its dimensions, the encoder has to spread 20 KB across 12 million pixels—causing blocky compression artifacts. Instead, select 600 × 600 px (Passport / Visa) or 800 px (Standard Form) in the Max Width / Height Preset dropdown first. With fewer pixels to encode, JPEG quality stays above 75%–85% even at 30 KB, keeping eyes, text, and signatures razor sharp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my passport photos or IDs uploaded to any server?

Never. Your image is decoded and re-encoded entirely inside your browser's local RAM using JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. You can even turn off Wi-Fi or mobile data after loading this page and compress photos completely offline.

Why is the compressed size slightly below my target (e.g., 47 KB for a 50 KB target)?

Online upload forms reject files that exceed their maximum byte limit by even 1 byte (50.1 KB fails a 50 KB check). Our algorithm intentionally targets the highest quality setting that stays safely under your KB ceiling so your upload passes on the first try.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting PNG to JPEG?

JPEG does not support transparency. When converting a transparent PNG to JPEG, SahilWebKit automatically fills transparent areas with a clean solid white background instead of turning them black. If you want to keep transparency, choose WebP or PNG.

Does compressing remove camera EXIF & GPS location metadata?

Yes. Re-drawing the image onto a fresh HTML5 Canvas strips hidden camera serial numbers and GPS coordinates automatically, reducing file size further and protecting your location privacy when sharing photos online.
