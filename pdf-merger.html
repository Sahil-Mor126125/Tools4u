Zero-Upload Document Security & Workflow Guide

How Browser-Based PDF Merging Works Without Uploading Your Files

Combining multiple PDF files—such as signed employment agreements, monthly VAT/tax invoices, mortgage disclosures, or multi-part academic transcripts—is an everyday necessity across legal, accounting, and administrative workflows. Yet most free online PDF tools force you to upload confidential files onto remote third-party servers before stitching them together. SahilWebKit eliminates that security bottleneck by performing the entire merge operation locally inside your web browser.

1. Why Zero-Upload Local Merging Protects Sensitive Documents

When you upload bank statements, client contracts, or medical records to a server-based PDF website, your documents travel over external networks and reside on cloud storage drives—often violating corporate NDA policies, GDPR data-minimization rules, or healthcare privacy standards.

SahilWebKit loads the open-source pdf-lib engine directly in your browser tab. When you drop files into the queue, JavaScript reads the raw binary ArrayBuffer in local RAM, extracts the exact page trees and embedded fonts you select, and writes a fresh single-file PDF blob straight to your Downloads folder. Zero bytes ever touch an external server.

2. Step-by-Step Workflow & Custom Page Range Tips

  • 1 Batch-Add Your PDFs: Drag and drop all files at once or add additional PDFs from different folders sequentially.
  • 2 Arrange Exact Sequence: Use the Move Up / Move Down arrows or click Sort A–Z to order numbered invoices or chapters cleanly.
  • 3 Filter Page Ranges (Optional): Type ranges like 1-2, 5 in any file's Page Range field to skip blank cover sheets or terms-and-conditions appendices.
  • 4 Sanitize & Export: Keep Strip Legacy Metadata enabled to wipe old creator software tags, then click Merge & Download PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are my PDF contracts, invoices, or bank statements uploaded to a server?

Never. Every PDF file is read and combined strictly inside your browser's local memory using JavaScript. You can even turn off your internet connection after loading this page and merge confidential documents offline.

2. Can I merge specific page ranges instead of entire PDF files?

Yes. Next to each queued PDF, use the Pages box to enter ranges like 1-3, 6. Leave it blank or type all to include every page in that file.

3. Why does a password-protected PDF show an encryption notice?

Encrypted bank or payroll PDFs lock their internal page streams with a cipher. To merge one, open it in Chrome or Adobe Reader, enter your password, choose Print → Save as PDF to save an unlocked copy, and add that unlocked file here.

4. Is there any limit on file size or number of PDFs?

There are no artificial file limits or daily quotas. Because merging runs on your device's own RAM, you can combine 10, 25, or 50+ PDFs in seconds while preserving original vector text sharpness and clickable hyperlinks.

5. What does "Strip Legacy Author & Producer Metadata" do?

PDF files often carry hidden document properties revealing the original employee's computer username, accounting software version, or internal keywords. Checking Strip Legacy Author & Producer Metadata resets those fields cleanly before generating your merged download.
