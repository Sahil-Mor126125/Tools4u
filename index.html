How PureWebKit Calculators & Zero-Upload Browser Tools Work

PureWebKit is designed around two core principles: transparent mathematical formulas for financial and clean-energy planning, and 100% client-side execution for image compression and PDF document processing. Every calculation and file transformation runs inside your browser's local memory without transmitting sensitive files or financial inputs to external cloud servers.

1. Off-Grid Solar & LiFePO4 Battery Sizing Math

When sizing an off-grid battery bank, nameplate amp-hours (Ah) cannot be drained completely without damaging cells. Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries safely deliver an 80% Depth of Discharge (DoD) over 4,000+ cycles, whereas sealed AGM/Gel lead-acid batteries should not exceed 50% DoD. We also divide daily AC watt-hours by a 92% inverter conversion efficiency factor so real-world parasitic draw never leaves your system undersized.

Battery Ah = ((Daily_Wh ÷ 0.92) × Autonomy_Days) ÷ (System_Volts × Safe_DoD)

2. SaaS LTV, Churn & CAC Payback Benchmarks

In subscription software businesses, raw revenue overstates customer value if hosting, support, and payment processing costs are ignored. PureWebKit computes Gross-Margin-Adjusted LTV by multiplying Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) by your Gross Margin percentage, divided by Monthly Logo Churn. Healthy venture-scale and bootstrapped SaaS businesses aim for an LTV:CAC ratio between 3.0x and 5.0x and a CAC payback window under 12 months.

LTV = (ARPU × Gross_Margin%) ÷ Monthly_Churn%  |  Payback = CAC ÷ (ARPU × Margin%)

3. EV Home Charging Efficiency & Off-Peak Tariffs

Electric vehicles draw roughly 10% more energy from a Level 2 home wallbox than reaches the battery pack due to onboard AC-to-DC rectifier losses and battery thermal management. Even after factoring in a 90% charging efficiency, pairing overnight Time-of-Use (TOU) rates ($0.08–$0.11/kWh in the US or 7p/kWh on UK smart EV tariffs) reduces per-mile energy expenditure by 70% to 80% compared to petrol/gasoline vehicles.

4. How Exact-KB Compression & Local PDF Merging Protect Privacy

Traditional online file converters upload your passport photos, tax returns, or signed contracts to remote servers. PureWebKit eliminates that privacy risk. Our Exact-KB Image Reducer renders your photo onto an isolated HTML5 <canvas> element and runs an iterative binary search across compression quality levels until the resulting binary blob fits strictly underneath your chosen KB ceiling. Similarly, our PDF Merger and Images-to-PDF Converter assemble raw PDF byte structures directly in browser RAM via pdf-lib.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDFs or images ever uploaded to a server?

No. All image resizing, KB compression, PDF page merging, and image-to-PDF conversions execute 100% locally on your device. If you disconnect from Wi-Fi after loading this page, every tool continues to work offline.

Why does PNG ignore quality sliders when targeting exact KB?

PNG is a lossless image format, so browsers ignore JPEG-style quality parameters. When PNG is selected and a file exceeds your KB target, PureWebKit automatically downscales pixel dimensions proportionally until the PNG meets your target size—or you can switch output format to JPEG/WebP for maximum sharpness at 20KB–100KB.

Does the currency switcher convert my custom inputs?

Switching between USD ($) and GBP (£) updates all currency labels and converts hardware benchmark estimates (like solar/battery equipment cost) automatically while preserving any custom tariff, ARPU, or fuel prices you typed in.

Can I bookmark or share a direct link to one specific tool?

Yes. Each calculator and file utility updates the URL hash (for example, #image-reducer or #pdf-merger). Click the Share button in the top bar to copy the direct link.
